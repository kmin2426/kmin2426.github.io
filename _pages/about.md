I am an undergraduate student in **Automotive Engineering at Hanyang University**, with a minor in **Data Science**, expecting to graduate in February 2027.

My interests lie in **autonomous driving, computer vision, and vision-language models**. For my bachelor's thesis, advised by Prof. Kichun Jo, I study efficient visual processing for vision-language models under constrained visual budgets.

Previously, I was a research intern with the **Vehicle Intelligence & Automated Driving Team at ika, RWTH Aachen University**, where I worked on detection evaluation and road-surface water segmentation. I also worked as an undergraduate researcher in the **Intelligent Robotics and Computer Vision Lab at Hanyang University**, advised by Prof. Soonmin Hwang, on traffic-light perception and field data acquisition.
